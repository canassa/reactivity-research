# 2026-10-16: work per event (`./rr work`)

Size alone has a loophole: an approach can save bytes by doing more at runtime (digest
re-evaluates every binding after every event). This session found a way to measure that.

## Method

For each framework and size, in headless Chrome on the serve page: take the DevTools
profiler's precise coverage (`Profiler.startPreciseCoverage({ callCount: true, detailed: true })`),
which resets the counters, perform one action the way the panel does, `await rr.settled()`,
take the coverage again and sum it over the scripts under `/<variant>/` (the built bundle; the
panel, its hooks and the reference spec are other URLs, and the evaluated step has no URL).
The actions (`harness/work/dashboard.js`) are the first widget of each kind plus `tick`,
`serverSet`, `notify` and a snapshot, so the same action touches the same widget at every size
(the generator is prefix-stable; checked: w1..w16 are identical at N=16, 64, 256). The
reference `update` only tracks the state to build messages in Node. Two fresh pages per count.

## What is robust

- **calls** (function invocations) with **TurboFan and Maglev off**
  (`--js-flags=--no-turbofan --no-maglev`): all 1,530 counts (6 frameworks × 5 sizes × 3 seeds ×
  17 actions) are identical across fresh pages, and across separate runs of the command.
- **blocks** (basic-block executions, summed over `detailed` ranges) give the same median
  ratios (1.00 for the local ones, 16 to 17 for digest and Elm); the worst local ratio is up to
  5.4 instead of 5.3, so the verdict does not hinge on which of the two is used. Calls is the
  headline: it is the easier number to explain. Blocks are stored in `work.json` too.

## What is not

- **With the optimizers on, counts are not usable.** Optimized code inlines callees, the
  inlined calls drop out of the count, and when code gets hot depends on warm-up. The same
  Solid action (toggles: flip one) cost 306 calls at N=16 and 166 at N=64, only because the
  larger page had warmed up further; Elm counts varied by 1-3% between identical runs
  (3550 vs 3547, 3056 vs 2957). With the optimizers off both effects are gone. This is the
  dead end that fixed the flags.
- Not tried: `Runtime.getHeapUsage` and allocation counts. GC timing makes them noisy, and the
  calls already separate the strategies cleanly; revisit if a strategy does its work without
  calling functions.
- The count is about work, not time: a native call such as `querySelectorAll` is one call
  however slow. A strategy that hides work in a few heavy built-ins would look cheaper than it
  is. Not seen yet.

## Results (dashboard, seeds 1-3, median over the 16 local actions)

| calls per action | N=16 | 32 | 64 | 128 | 256 | N=256 / N=16, worst local action over seeds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| digest | 283 | 504 | 1,119 | 2,236 | 4,521 | 18.5 |
| elm | 3,848 | 7,556 | 15,900 | 32,053 | 64,640 | 17.3 |
| solid | 162 | 166 | 172 | 191 | 191 | 5.0 |
| tea-dirtybits | 25 | 25 | 25 | 27 | 27 | 5.3 |
| tea-inline | 17 | 18 | 19 | 20 | 20 | 3.2 |
| tea-local | 24 | 24 | 24 | 32 | 33 | 4.2 |

The snapshot is global by nature (it replaces every widget); every strategy grows 15 to 16.4x
on it, a sanity check that the count sees real work.

- **Global:** digest (about 18 extra calls per extra widget on every action) and Elm (about 253:
  the whole view is re-rendered and diffed). Ratios of 16 to 18 for a 16x larger page: linear.
- **Local:** Solid and the three tea prototypes.
- The residual growth of the local ones is in few actions and is real, not noise. `tick`
  scans every widget for running clocks (the reference does too), and a widget that more
  summaries read costs more as N grows: summaries are 1 per 8 widgets and pick their sources
  among earlier widgets, so w3 has 1 reader at N=16 and 2 at N=256 (amount, filter, toggles,
  rating, tick grow for that reason). That is dependents growing, not whole-page work.

## Proposed rule (not wired into `check`)

> **Locality.** On the prototype track, for every local action of the scenario's work script,
> calls at the largest size (N=256) must be at most **8x** the calls at the smallest (N=16),
> for each seed. Actions that replace the whole state (the snapshot) are exempt.

The size grows 16x, so 8x means "at most half-linear". Every local action of every
non-global approach stays at or below 5.3x (tea-dirtybits, `tick`); every global one reaches 14.9x
or more on its least affected local action and 16 to 18.5x on its worst. Nothing falls between
5.3x and 14.9x, so k anywhere in 6 to 14 gives the same verdict; 8 leaves margin on both sides.
Pass: solid, tea-local, tea-inline, tea-dirtybits. Fail: digest, elm (Elm is not a prototype,
so the rule would not apply to it; it fails as expected). One scenario with one script is a thin
base: re-derive k when another scenario or a new strategy arrives.
